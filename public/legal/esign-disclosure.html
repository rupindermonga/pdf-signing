Electronic Records and Signatures Disclosure

Version 2026-04-24. Last updated 2026-04-24.

Before you sign, please read and confirm your consent. US federal law (the Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act, 15 U.S.C. §7001, "ESIGN") and most US state laws (the Uniform Electronic Transactions Act, "UETA") require us to give you this disclosure and obtain your consent before we can use electronic records and signatures in our transactions with you.

1. Scope of consent

By clicking "I agree and consent" on the signing page, you agree that any document we present to you through the CertaDocs platform may be delivered, reviewed, signed, and stored in electronic form, and that your electronic signature is the legal equivalent of your manual, handwritten signature.

2. Your right to withdraw consent

You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting the sender who requested your signature or by emailing support@finelai.com. Withdrawing consent will not affect the legal validity of records or signatures you provided before withdrawal. After withdrawal, any document you were asked to sign may no longer be available in electronic form, and the sender may need to re-initiate the transaction on paper.

3. Paper copies

You have the right to receive a paper copy of any document signed through CertaDocs. Request one from the document sender or by emailing support@finelai.com. Paper copies may incur a reasonable reproduction fee.

4. Hardware and software requirements

  • A current version of Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge
  • An active email address and the ability to access it
  • A device with at least 1 GB of RAM and a connection to the internet
  • A touchscreen, mouse, or trackpad for drawing your signature
  • Ability to view PDF files (handled in-browser by CertaDocs)

5. Updating your contact information

If your email or phone number changes, update it with the party that sent you the document for signature.

6. Legal effect

Your electronic signature is legally binding. In jurisdictions outside the United States, equivalent laws apply: PIPEDA (Canada), Quebec Law 25 (Quebec), the Personal Data Protection Act 2023 ("DPDP Act") and the Information Technology Act 2000 (India), the Electronic Transactions Act 1999 (Australia), and Regulation (EU) No 910/2014 "eIDAS" (European Union). CertaDocs electronic signatures are designed to satisfy "simple" and "advanced" electronic signature tiers under eIDAS; "qualified" electronic signatures (QES) require a Qualified Trust Service Provider certificate, which your organisation must configure separately.

7. Retention

Signed documents, along with an audit trail containing IP address, browser information, timestamps, and consent events, are retained by the organisation that sent the document for signature. Your employer or the sending party determines the retention period; contact them for access, correction, export, or deletion of your data.
