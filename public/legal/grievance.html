Grievance Officer / Data-Subject Requests

In accordance with §10 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (India) and equivalent rights under PIPEDA (Canada), GDPR (EU), the Privacy Act (Australia), and state laws (US), SealForge provides the following channel for grievances and data-subject requests.

Contact the Grievance Officer (India – DPDP Act 2023)

Email: grievance@finelai.com

Response time: We acknowledge within 48 hours and resolve within 30 days, per §10(1) DPDP Act.

Privacy Officer (Canada – PIPEDA / Quebec Law 25)

Email: privacy@finelai.com

Escalation: Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada — priv.gc.ca, or Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec.

Data Protection Officer (EU – GDPR)

Email: dpo@finelai.com
