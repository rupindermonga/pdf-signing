Privacy Policy

Version 2026-04-24. Applies globally with jurisdiction-specific addenda below.

This Privacy Policy describes how SealForge ("we", "us") collects, uses, and discloses personal information when you use the SealForge platform. SealForge is a multi-tenant PDF signature platform; in most cases your employer, counterparty, or service provider (the "Sender") is the data controller of documents you sign, and we act as a data processor on their behalf.

1. What we collect

CategoryExamplesPurpose
IdentityName, email, phoneDeliver documents, verify identity
AuditIP, user agent, timestamps, approximate locationLegal evidence of signing (ESIGN, eIDAS, IT Act)

Optional IDPhoto of government ID, selfieOnly if the Sender requires identity verification
Optional paymentStripe/Razorpay transaction IDOnly if the Sender collects payment

2. How we use it

  • To perform the signing ceremony you consented to
  • To produce the audit trail and certificate of completion
  • To comply with legal obligations (e-signature laws, tax law, anti-fraud)
  • To notify you and the Sender of status changes
  • Never to advertise to you or sell your data

3. How we store it

Documents and audit trails are stored encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM for uploaded IDs). TLS in transit. Database in the region the Sender's organisation has configured (see §8).

4. Retention

Retention is set by the Sender's organisation (default: indefinite). When retention expires, personal identifiers are pseudonymised and documents are purged. Audit records are retained as required by law (typically 7 years).

5. Your rights

You may:

  • Request a copy of your personal data (access / portability)
  • Request correction of inaccurate data (rectification)
  • Request deletion (erasure) — audit records may be retained in pseudonymised form where law requires
  • Withdraw your consent for electronic signing (future transactions only)
  • Lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority

Exercise any of these rights by emailing privacy@finelai.com or via the data-subject request form. We respond within 30 days (GDPR/PIPEDA) or as required by local law.

6. Third-party processors

  • Email: your SMTP provider / our configured provider
  • SMS: Twilio (optional, if enabled)
  • Payments: Stripe (cards), Razorpay (INR)
  • Identity verification: Persona / Jumio / Onfido / Interac (only if Sender enables)
  • Aadhaar eSign: eMudhra or Protean/NSDL (India, only if used)
  • Timestamping (TSA): per organisation configuration

7. Children

SealForge is not intended for persons under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

8. Jurisdictional addenda

Canada (PIPEDA)

The Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act governs our handling of personal information of Canadian residents. Our accountability contact is privacy@finelai.com. Data residency in Canada (AWS ca-central-1 / Azure Canada Central) is available on request. For Canadian residents, you may also file a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada at priv.gc.ca.

Quebec (Law 25)

Our privacy officer for Quebec residents is privacy@finelai.com. Québec residents may exercise the rights of access, rectification, deportability, and de-indexing granted by Law 25. We conduct Privacy Impact Assessments for high-risk features (e.g. ID verification).

India (DPDP Act 2023)

Our grievance officer, appointed under §10(1) of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, is contactable at grievance@finelai.com. We respond to grievances within 30 days. You may also escalate to the Data Protection Board of India.

European Union (GDPR)

Legal bases: Art 6(1)(b) contract, Art 6(1)(c) legal obligation, Art 6(1)(f) legitimate interests (audit integrity). Data Protection Officer: dpo@finelai.com. EU data residency in Frankfurt (eu-central-1) available. SCCs / UK IDTA for cross-border transfers.

Australia (Privacy Act / APPs)

We handle personal information consistent with the Australian Privacy Principles. Complaints may be made to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

United States

For California residents (CCPA/CPRA): we do not sell or share your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. You have the right to know, delete, correct, and limit. Request these at privacy@finelai.com.
